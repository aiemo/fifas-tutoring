Policies

  • Payments must be made at least 24 hours before the scheduled session, or the session will be cancelled.
  • Afifa reserves the right to decline any tutoring request; no disrespect will be tolerated.
  • Zelle is preferred for payments, though other arrangements can be made upon request.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance will receive a 50% refund of the session fee.
  • No-shows will not receive a refund.
  • Unused sessions may be refunded with written notice.
  • Dissatisfaction with a grade does not automatically qualify for a refund.
  • Duplicate or accidental payments will be refunded.
  • Sessions are held via Zoom. Cameras are optional and based on student comfort, but a working microphone and reliable internet connection are required.
  • Sessions will not be recorded unless the tutor, student, and (if applicable) parent/guardian provide written consent. Recordings incur an additional $10 fee.
  • For students under 18: a parent or guardian must complete registration, handle payment, and be included in scheduling and other communications.
  • Materials and copyright: all worksheets, slides, and study guides are intended solely for enrolled, paying students and may not be sold, shared, or redistributed.

Subject Details

Click a subject to see what's covered.

Undergraduate General Chemistry

Comprehensive support covering the full general chemistry sequence: atomic structure, periodic trends, chemical bonding, stoichiometry, gases, thermochemistry, kinetics, equilibrium, acids and bases, solubility, and electrochemistry.

Undergraduate Organic Chemistry

In-depth help across the full organic chemistry sequence: structure and bonding, stereochemistry, acids and bases, reaction mechanisms, substitution and elimination reactions, alkene and alkyne reactions, aromatic chemistry, alcohols and ethers, carbonyl chemistry, carboxylic acid derivatives, and spectroscopy.

Undergraduate Biology

Support across core biology topics including general biology, cell biology, genetics, biochemistry, molecular biology, ecology, evolution, human physiology, and microbiology.

Undergraduate Physics

Coverage of the full physics sequence: mechanics, kinematics, forces, work and energy, momentum, rotation and torque, fluids, thermodynamics, waves and sound, electricity and circuits, magnetism, and optics.

AP Biology

Exam-focused preparation for AP Biology, including concept review, practice questions, and study strategies aligned with the College Board curriculum.

AP Chemistry

Exam-focused preparation for AP Chemistry, including concept review, practice questions, and study strategies aligned with the College Board curriculum.

AP History

Support for AP History courses including APUSH (AP U.S. History) and AP World History, with practice worksheets and strategies tailored to the College Board exams.

Middle School Math

Foundational math support to build confidence and strengthen core skills for middle school students.

Middle School History

Support with middle school history and social studies coursework, including review and study strategies.

Middle School Science

Support with middle school science coursework, including review and study strategies.

College Application Essay Assistance

Guidance on brainstorming, structuring, and refining college application essays to help students tell their story clearly and effectively.
